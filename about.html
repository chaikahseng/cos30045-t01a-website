Our Mission

Making Data Accessible.

Dedicated to making energy consumption data accessible and visually engaging. We transform complex datasets into actionable insights for everyday Australians.

The Project

This project is developed as part of the COS30045 Data Visualisation unit. Our goal is to transform raw, complex data regarding Australian appliance energy consumption into intuitive, interactive visualizations. We believe that when people can see their energy use clearly, they can make better choices.
